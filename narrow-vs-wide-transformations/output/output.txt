========================================================================
NARROW vs WIDE TRANSFORMATIONS - does it shuffle?
A shuffle moves data across the cluster. It is the expensive part,
and in the physical plan it shows up as an "Exchange".
========================================================================

NARROW - each input partition feeds one output, no shuffle:
  filter         no shuffle
  select         no shuffle
  withColumn     no shuffle
  union          no shuffle
  coalesce       no shuffle

WIDE - needs a shuffle (data crosses the network):
  groupBy + agg  shuffle
  distinct       shuffle
  orderBy        shuffle
  join           shuffle
  repartition    shuffle

------------------------------------------------------------------------
See it in the plan - the wide one has an Exchange, the narrow one doesn't:

  filter (narrow):
     *(1) Filter (isnotnull(amount#2L) AND (amount#2L > 100))
     +- *(1) Scan ExistingRDD[region#0,product#1,amount#2L]

  groupBy (wide):
     +- HashAggregate(keys=[region#0], functions=[sum(amount#2L)], output=[region#0, sum(amount)#21L])
        +- Exchange hashpartitioning(region#0, 4), ENSURE_REQUIREMENTS, [plan_id=*]
           +- HashAggregate(keys=[region#0], functions=[partial_sum(amount#2L)], output=[region#0, sum#23L])
              +- Project [region#0, amount#2L]
                 +- Scan ExistingRDD[region#0,product#1,amount#2L]

------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE TRAP - these two look almost identical, but only one shuffles:

  df.coalesce(2)    (narrow - just merges partitions in place)
     Coalesce 2
     +- *(1) Scan ExistingRDD[region#0,product#1,amount#2L]

  df.repartition(2) (wide - a full shuffle)
     +- Exchange RoundRobinPartitioning(2), REPARTITION_BY_NUM, [plan_id=*]
        +- Scan ExistingRDD[region#0,product#1,amount#2L]

========================================================================
The rule: if it shuffles, Spark writes to disk and moves data over the
network - that's a stage boundary, and it's what makes jobs slow at scale.
Narrow chains pipeline together for free. Everything else is detail.
========================================================================
