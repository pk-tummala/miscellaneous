==========================================================================
CONDITIONAL SALTING: salt only the skewed key, not the whole table
==========================================================================
orders (2,000,000) JOIN customers (4,001) ON customer_id.

--- 1. The skew ---
  ACME_CORP owns 1,600,000 of 2,000,000 orders (80%).
  A plain join puts all 1,600,000 on one task - the straggler.

--- 2. Find skewed keys dynamically (count > 10,000) ---
  skewed keys: ['ACME_CORP']

--- 3. NORMAL salting: salt EVERY key, fan out the WHOLE dimension ---
  salt = order_id % 16 for all orders; explode ALL 4,001 customers x 16
  customers dimension: 4,001 -> 64,016 rows   (16x bigger)
  busiest join task: 100,000 rows   (skew fixed)

--- 4. CONDITIONAL salting: salt ONLY the skewed keys ---
  salt = order_id % 16 for skewed keys; salt = 0 for everyone else
  explode ONLY skewed rows x 16; the 4,000 normal customers pass through
  customers dimension: 4,001 -> 4,016 rows   (+15)
  busiest join task: 100,000 rows   (skew fixed)

--- 5. Same fix, far less overhead ---
  both fix the skew:    busiest task 1,600,000 -> 100,000
  dimension shuffled:   normal 64,016  vs  conditional 4,016  (~16x smaller)
  join result:          2,000,000 rows, identical: True
