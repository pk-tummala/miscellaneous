==========================================================================
BROADCAST JOINS - stop shuffling the big side
==========================================================================

fact  = 2,000,000 rows   (the big side)
dim   =       200 rows   (the small side)
join  = fact JOIN dim ON dim_id

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Regular join (dimension above the broadcast threshold) -> SORT-MERGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   *(5) SortMergeJoin [dim_id], [dim_id], Inner
   +- Exchange hashpartitioning(dim_id, 8), ENSURE_REQUIREMENTS
   Exchange hashpartitioning(dim_id, 8), ENSURE_REQUIREMENTS

   Both sides get an Exchange (hash-partitioned by dim_id) - i.e. BOTH
   tables are shuffled across the network before they can be joined.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Broadcast the small side -> BROADCAST HASH JOIN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   fact.join(broadcast(dim), "dim_id")
   *(2) BroadcastHashJoin [dim_id], [dim_id], Inner, BuildRight, false, false
   BroadcastExchange HashedRelationBroadcastMode(List(input[0, bigint, false]),false)

   Only the dim gets a BroadcastExchange (sent to every executor). There
   is NO Exchange on the fact - the 2,000,000-row side never moves.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Same answer either way: sort-merge 2,000,000 rows == broadcast 2,000,000 rows
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

==========================================================================
A broadcast join trades network for memory: ship the small side once to
each executor so the big side isn't shuffled. Spark auto-broadcasts under
spark.sql.autoBroadcastJoinThreshold (default 10MB); broadcast() forces it.
Ceiling: Spark cannot broadcast a relation larger than 8GB, and the small
side is collected to the DRIVER first - so 'small' means small.
==========================================================================
